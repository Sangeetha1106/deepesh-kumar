Frequently Asked Corporate Questions

How do you customize programs for our specific industry culture?

Every engagement begins with a comprehensive discovery phase where we interview stakeholders, review internal competency benchmarks, and craft custom case studies reflecting your team's real challenges.

What is the optimal cohort size for your workshops?

For deep behavioural workshops, 15 to 25 participants ensures maximum psychological safety and active peer engagement. For strategic keynotes and town halls, audiences can scale to several hundreds.

How is training ROI and behavioural shift measured?

We incorporate pre- and post-workshop pulse surveys, 30-day implementation check-ins, and manager feedback audits to verify tangible behavioral adoption on the job.

Do you offer pan-India and hybrid delivery options?

Yes. Deepesh conducts in-person workshops across all major enterprise hubs in India (Bengaluru, Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Hyderabad, Pune) as well as virtual cohorts for globally distributed teams.
